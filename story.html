THE VOGEL TIMEPIECE STORY

Some Things Are Worth Preserving.

A passion for history became a life dedicated to the preservation of fine timepieces.

WHERE IT BEGAN

A Watch Is More Than a Machine.

For Gustav Vogel, the fascination with antique timepieces comes from something deeper than their ability to tell time.

Every watch reflects the people, craftsmanship, technology, and culture of the period in which it was created. The smallest mechanical details can reveal a great deal about the history surrounding a timepiece.

BETWEEN TWO HOMES

San Francisco & Mühlhausen.

Gustav's connection to both San Francisco and Mühlhausen, Germany gives his work a unique relationship with history and tradition.

THE BUSINESS

Vogel Timepiece.

Vogel Timepiece was created around the belief that rare and antique watches deserve careful attention rather than being treated as disposable objects.

Gustav's work focuses on repairing and restoring antique and rare timepieces while respecting the craftsmanship that went into creating them.

The goal is simple: preserve the function, character, and story of each timepiece so that it can continue into another generation.

"
A timepiece should not simply tell you what time it is. It should remind you where it has been.

— Gustav Vogel
