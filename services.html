OUR SERVICES

Craftsmanship With a Respect for History.

Every antique timepiece presents its own challenges. Vogel Timepiece approaches each piece individually, considering its age, condition, construction, and history before beginning any restoration or repair.

01

RESTORATION

Antique Watch Restoration

Careful restoration of antique and rare timepieces with an emphasis on preserving the character and craftsmanship of the original watch.

02

REPAIR

Mechanical Watch Repair

Detailed repair and adjustment of mechanical timepieces to help restore reliable function while respecting the original movement.

03

MAINTENANCE

Vintage Timepiece Care

Preventative care and maintenance for owners who want to preserve the condition and longevity of their vintage watches.

04

DESIGN

Timepiece Design

Thoughtful timepiece concepts inspired by traditional watchmaking and the mechanical artistry of historic watches.
