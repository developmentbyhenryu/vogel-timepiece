THE MAN BEHIND THE CRAFT

Preserving More Than Time.

Gustav Vogel is a 68-year-old watchmaker and owner of Vogel Timepiece. His work specializes in the repair and design of antique and rare timepieces.

Gustav divides his connection to home between San Francisco, California, and Mühlhausen, Germany, where his family home remains an important part of his personal history.

His background in World History has shaped the way he views antique watches. To Gustav, a timepiece is not simply a machine. It represents craftsmanship, history, and the people who owned it before.

A PHILOSOPHY OF CRAFTSMANSHIP

"Every timepiece deserves to be understood."

Vogel Timepiece approaches restoration with patience and respect for the character of each watch. The objective is not simply to make an old watch work again, but to preserve the craftsmanship that made it worth keeping.
